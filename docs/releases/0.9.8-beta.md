# AudioFreedom 0.9.8 Beta

This release adds an optional extended EQ range, a larger theme collection with a
visual gallery, and a clearer driver-update workflow.

## Extended Equalizer Range

- Added an Extended EQ range switch inside Equalizer, available in every theme.
- Standard range remains -12 to +12 dB. The optional unlocked range is -20 to +20 dB.
- First unlock requires acknowledging the distortion, high-volume, and equipment-damage warning.
- Unlocking preserves current gains and preamp. Turning it off immediately caps bands to +/-12 dB; gains already in range stay unchanged.
- Curves, faders, fine controls, accessibility controls, profiles, A/B recall, snapshots, and assistant changes respect the selected range.
- The unlock is a device preference, not a profile setting. Other effects retain their existing limits.
- Updated all three native backends to support the extended EQ range.

## Themes And Meters

- Choose from 12 themes: Classic, Mocha, Daylight, Studio, Dynamic, Media Player, OLED Black, Phosphor, Paper, Valve, Neon, and Cassette Deck.
- Settings > Themes opens a scrollable gallery with previews rendered from the actual app components. Tap a preview to apply its theme immediately.
- System, Light, and Dark controls remain visible above the gallery while browsing. Single-shade themes keep their intended light or dark appearance.
- Added distinctive meter treatments, including Phosphor's scope trace, Neon's glowing spectrum, and Valve's analog-style VU meter.
- Extended Valve's visual VU scale down to -30, so quieter audio moves the needle; audio gain and the 0 VU reference are unchanged.
- Cassette Deck now has a recessed cassette window, signal-driven reels, a metal faceplate, amber meters, and a working power key.
- Refined the Mocha and Daylight spectrum display with interpolated bars; the underlying analyzer still measures ten bands.

## Driver Updates

- Added a one-time main-screen reminder when the installed driver is older than the bundled module, with a shortcut to its update controls.
- Successful in-app module updates offer a confirmed phone restart. Settings keeps a restart-required state until the next boot instead of continuing to show Update.
- Extended EQ shows a driver warning only when an older, incompatible module is detected, not permanently on current drivers.

## Media Player Voice Replies

- Successful music requests say "Playing" by default; Detailed music replies restores the artist/category and track-count reply.
- Music forwarding and reply settings are visible in both Push-to-talk and Volume-button modes.

## Updating And Installation

**This release includes a new driver module. Update it and restart your phone to use
extended EQ gains.** Older drivers cap EQ at +/-12 dB.

1. Install AudioFreedom-0.9.8-beta.apk over your existing app. Settings and profiles are retained.
2. Open Settings > Updates > Driver module and install the bundled 0.9.8-beta module.
3. After successful installation, choose Restart now, or restart later using the phone's power menu.
4. Reopen AudioFreedom and enable processing. Extended EQ remains opt-in inside Equalizer.

The APK includes the matching universal driver ZIP; no separate download is needed
for in-app installation. AudioFreedom-universal-0.9.8-beta.zip is also attached for
manual installation through Magisk, KernelSU, or a compatible root-module manager.
Do not install the module twice; choose either method.

Start at low playback volume. Boosts cannot restore missing information or overcome
the physical limits of a speaker. Output limiting is not a guarantee against equipment damage.

## Validation

185 app unit tests passed, alongside targeted emulator tests for theme selection,
EQ controls and persistence, meters, and the driver-update/restart UI. The extended
EQ driver also passed native engine, Volume Leveler, and playback-analysis tests.
