# Bass Foundation and Immersive Field test

## Installation

1. Install `AudioFreedom-xuanyuan-eea-0.9.0-foundation1-directbind.zip` in KernelSU or
   Magisk and reboot.
2. Install `AudioFreedom-0.9.0-foundation1-debug.apk`.
3. Keep Output protection enabled with a `-1.0 dB` ceiling during initial testing.

## Bass Foundation

1. Disable EQ, Detail recovery, and Immersive field.
2. For full-size headphones, select `Balanced headphones` and start at 35% strength.
3. Compare Processing on/off, then try 50% and 65%.
4. For earbuds or the phone speaker, choose the matching preset and compare
   Small-driver support at 0%, 50%, and the preset value.
5. Listen for bass below the kick drum, less strain on loud bass hits, and additional
   audible pitch on small drivers without buzzing or vocal coloration.
6. Reduce Strength or Bass range if the limiter remains active or upper bass becomes
   heavy.

## Immersive Field

1. Disable Bass Foundation and Detail recovery for the first comparison.
2. Start with `Wide music`, then toggle Immersive field while a clearly stereo track is
   playing.
3. Compare `Natural stage`, `Wide music`, and `Cinema`.
4. Check that vocals remain centered, the stage expands beyond the headphones, and
   transients do not become hollow or phasey.
5. Repeat with mono speech. Strong coloration or unstable center placement is a defect.
